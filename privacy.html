Daily Dispatch - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Daily Dispatch is a small personal app that automatically posts content (jokes, public-domain NASA clips, and "on this day" history cards) to Facebook Pages owned and managed by its developer, Mike O'Brien.

Information we collect

Daily Dispatch does not collect, store, or process personal information from visitors, followers, or anyone who views or interacts with the Pages. The app has no sign-up, no login for the public, and no website forms. It only publishes posts to Pages administered by its developer.

How information is used

Because no visitor data is collected, none is used, sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties.

Third-party services

Posts are published through Meta's (Facebook's) platform. Meta's own privacy policy governs any information you share with Facebook when you view or interact with a Page.

Data deletion

Daily Dispatch does not store personal data about users, so there is nothing to delete. If you believe the app holds any information about you, email obrienmd2@yahoo.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and it will be removed within 30 days.
